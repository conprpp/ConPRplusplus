ConPR++: A Multi-Site Indoor-Outdoor Construction Dataset for Place Recognition under Progressive Structural Change

Code Data

Abstract

Long-term place recognition (PR) remains challenging in environments where the scene itself evolves over time. While existing benchmarks primarily consider appearance variations from illumination, weather, and seasonal changes, far fewer datasets capture persistent geometric changes induced by construction. To address this gap, we introduce ConPR++, a multi-session dataset for place recognition in real-world construction environments. It comprises 22 recording sessions across three indoor and outdoor construction sites, spanning several weeks to months. The dataset provides synchronized camera images, LiDAR point clouds, IMU measurements, and ground-truth poses, capturing structural evolution, viewpoint and illumination changes, and dynamic objects. We further propose the Visually Grounded Temporal Change Ratio (TCRVG), which combines geometric and appearance differences and shows stronger correlations with visual and multimodal PR performance than geometry-only metrics. Benchmarking 12 LiDAR, visual, and fusion methods reveals considerable room for improving multimodal fusion. ConPR++ enables systematic evaluation of visual, LiDAR-based, and multimodal place recognition under construction-induced changes.
